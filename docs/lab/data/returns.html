Returns and repairs

You may return anything unused within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Start a return from your order page; the label is free.

Faulty gear

If a zip breaks, a seam opens or a buckle snaps within two years, we repair or replace it free of charge. Send us a photo of the fault with your order number.

Sizing

Boots and jackets that don't fit may be exchanged for another size within 60 days, worn or not, as long as they're clean.

Refunds

Refunds reach your card within five working days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
